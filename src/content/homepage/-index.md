overview:
  title: From shared interests to shared practice
  content: The first NeuroHCI meet-up at CHI 2026 brought together nearly 100 participants. This proposed follow-up focuses on how researchers study, build, measure, and intervene across brain, body, and interaction. Through methodology mapping, small-group networking, and an informal exchange of current work, participants will encounter complementary approaches, discuss shared challenges, and identify opportunities for collaboration. Researchers, practitioners, and newcomers are welcome; no prior NeuroHCI expertise is required.

agenda:
  title: What we’ll do
  description: A proposed 90-minute session. Times below are minutes from the start of the meet-up.
  items:
    - time: 0–10 min
      title: Welcome & Methodology Mapping
      description: Following a short introduction, choose stickers representing methods you use or want to discuss. The stickers will help start conversations and identify people using complementary approaches.
    - time: 10–50 min
      title: NeuroHCI Networking Rounds
      description: Rotate between groups of three, with about three minutes per person to introduce yourself and share a current project, research question, or area of work. Organizers will help form groups and facilitate rotations.
    - time: 50–80 min
      title: NeuroHCI in Practice Exchange
      description: Informally share and experience current work, moving between lightweight prototypes, sensing or stimulation systems, interaction demos, methodological tools, videos, and discussions. These examples will showcase relevant work and serve as informal conversation prompts.
    - time: 80–90 min
      title: Group Summary
      description: "Share one takeaway in the NeuroHCI Slack channel, optionally with an image: something you learned, a method or project you encountered, or a new connection. These posts will support continued discussion."
